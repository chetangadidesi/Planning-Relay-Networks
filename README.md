# Planning-Relay-Networks
Planning Relay Networks That Avoid Enemy Line-of-Sight and Maximize Angle Between Links and Enemy

# Abstract
Unmanned aerial vehicle relay networks enable
beyond-line-of-sight communication in contested environments,
but are vulnerable to electronic surveillance and localization.
This paper proposes a method to synthesize relay networks that
prioritize concealment through terrain masking and adaptive 3D
beam-nulling. We leverage high-fidelity digital elevation models
and synthetic urban data from OpenStreetMap to develop a
pathfinding architecture that ensures communication beams main-
tain a maximum angular separation from enemy observers. We
evaluate three strategies: Shadow-Priority BFS, User-Constrained
Min-Link, and Max-Min Bottleneck Search
